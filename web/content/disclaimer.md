### Disclaimer: Experimental research product

The optical turbulence (OT) forecasts and the climatology are experimental research products.
The products serve as technology demonstrators and to give a qualitative, order-of-magnitude 
idea about the spatio-temporal evolution of OT in the Netherlands. 

Do not use the products for operational decisions without validating them with observations.
Data is licensed as [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), which excludes any warranty. 

Additional notes:

- OTProf is trained on model output from the Weather Research and Forecasting (WRF) model **not** on observations. WRF is known to have biases and errors compared to observations, which are inherited by OTProf. 
- Compared to our [publication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09346), forecasts are derived from GFS data instead of ERA5 data. GFS is produced by a different numerical weather model with different errors and biases. We did not assess performance on GFS data but expect it to be qualitatively acceptable.
- Carefully check the performance discussion of OTProf in our [publication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09346) before using any data from this website.
