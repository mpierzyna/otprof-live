## About

Forecasts of the refractive index structure parameter Cₙ² from **SQF-H**, a SqueezeFormer trained on WRF
simulations over the Netherlands, driven by GFS forecasts and corrected with the nl output quantile mapping. r₀ and
σᵢ² are integrated over the whole column (vertical path from the ground).

Edit `web/public/notes.md` to change this text: it deploys with the next push.

Links can set the view, e.g. [Cabauw, 1 km, +12 h](#lat=51.97&lon=4.93&z=1000&t=12) or
[r₀ over the North Sea](#var=r0&lat=53.25&lon=4.25).
